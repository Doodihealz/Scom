Balance Rules

1. Do not use time-dependent formers. Optimization decisions must depend on the game state, available resources, or the enemy state.

2. Keep `F123 Land Primary` at `AttackEnemyStrength = 80`. Tests with 80, 90, and 100 showed that 80 achieves the fastest victories.

3. The AI must avoid reaching full Alloy storage. Alloy income received while storage is full is permanently lost and can never be recovered, causing an irreversible economy loss. Full Alloy storage indicates that sufficient energy production was established too late. The required energy production must already be available before Alloy storage can become full so that available Alloy can be spent continuously.

4. Whenever a former is changed and a condition must be retained to preserve a verified result, document that condition here before completing the patch. Record the former name, the required behavior or dependency, its purpose, and the supporting map/setup/log. Preserve those constraints during unrelated optimization. A deliberate replacement needs a documented reason and comparative tests covering the previously successful configuration. Distinguish required behavior from provisional tuning values; one favorable game is not proof of an optimal threshold. AI Patchnotes.txt is for players: record concise English descriptions of gameplay changes, fixes and balance adjustments under the appropriate release. Do not include internal test reports, log paths, profiling data, debug details or work-in-progress notes, and do not create a separate development journal unless requested.
